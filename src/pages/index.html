01

Programs

Three processes. One trade.

Choose a welding process to see how it works and where it is used.

{{pic key="h1-mig" alt="Close view of a welder in a helmet making a MIG weld, with a shower of sparks" sizes="(min-width:1024px) 55vw, 100vw" pos="50% 35%"}}

How it works

A wire electrode feeds continuously through the gun while shielding gas protects the weld. It is fast, steady and well suited to learning the fundamentals of arc control.

Typically used for

Fabrication shops, production welding, automotive and general repair, and structural assemblies.

MIG program details
{{pic key="h1-tig" alt="Gloved hands guiding a TIG torch and filler rod while a welder in a helmet works on a joint" sizes="(min-width:1024px) 55vw, 100vw" pos="50% 40%"}}

How it works

A non-consumable tungsten electrode makes the arc while you add filler rod by hand. It is slower and demands steady hands, and it gives the most control.

Typically used for

Stainless steel and aluminum work, thin material, piping, and welds where appearance and precision matter.

TIG program details
{{pic key="h1-stick" alt="A welder in a helmet and heavy jacket running a stick weld on a steel plate, with sparks" sizes="(min-width:1024px) 55vw, 100vw" pos="50% 45%"}}

How it works

A flux-coated electrode carries its own shielding, so no gas bottle is needed. The equipment is simple and rugged, and it handles wind, rust and rough conditions.

Typically used for

Structural steel, outdoor and field work, heavy equipment, and maintenance and repair.

03

Certification

A credential that shows what you can weld.

Certification is a documented test of your work. The program is built around preparing you for it, and your progress stays visible from enrollment to results.

  1. 1

    Enroll

    Pick a certification program and enroll through the student portal.

  2. 2

    Train and log hours

    Work at the booth. Your lab hours are tracked as you complete them.

  3. 3

    Prepare

    Practice for the assessment with instructor guidance.

  4. 4

    Take the assessment

    Your work is evaluated against the certification requirements.

  5. 5

    See your results

    Exam results are available in your student dashboard.

Certification details vary by program.
