Who we are

A trade school for welding.

Students come here to learn welding as a working skill. They train in MIG, TIG and Stick welding in the lab, prepare for certification, and keep a record of their progress in the student portal.

Our training philosophy

Skill lives in your hands.

Welding is learned by doing. Reading about an arc helps; running hundreds of beads is what builds control.

  1. 01

    Practice is the lesson

    Most of what you learn happens with a torch, gun or electrode in your hand.

  2. 02

    Feedback while it counts

    Technique is corrected during practice, before a habit sets.

  3. 03

    Progress you can see

    Lab hours and exam results are recorded in the student portal.

Learning the welding trade

Three ways to strike an arc.

Training covers the three core arc-welding processes. Each has its own feel, its own equipment and its own place in the trade.

  1. {{pic key="ab-mig" alt="A welder in a helmet making a weld on a steel part, with light from the arc and sparks" sizes="(min-width:768px) 33vw, 100vw" pos="45% 50%"}}

    01 MIG / GMAW

    Wire fed through a gun, shielded by gas. Fast and steady.

    MIG on the Programs page
  2. {{pic key="ab-tig" alt="A trainee in a welding helmet and orange jacket TIG welding a small part on a bench during training" sizes="(min-width:768px) 33vw, 100vw" pos="62% 50%"}}

    02 TIG / GTAW

    A tungsten arc with filler added by hand. Slow and precise.

    03 Stick / SMAW

    A flux-coated electrode that shields itself. Simple and rugged.
